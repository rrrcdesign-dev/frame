Medal Display Frame

8 Medals · 4×2 · 414 × 275 × 46 mm
01

Position medals and thread the slots

Place each medal below its slot on the front of the 4×2 middle board and thread the ribbon front-to-back through the horizontal slot.

02

Tape ribbons flat on the back

Flip the middle board over and secure the excess ribbon flat against the back face with masking tape.

03

Peel the cover

Peel the protective film off both sides of the 2 mm clear acrylic cover.

04

Push, click, and lock

With the frame facing down, stack the clear acrylic cover, front spacer bars, loaded middle board (face down), and backboard inside the frame.

05

Hang straight on the wall

Hook the frame's triple-thickness rear perimeter lip straight down onto a single nail centered in the wall.
